Block ships shaders from the GShade project (https://github.com/Mortalitas/GShade)
and the ReShade-compatible subset (https://github.com/Mortalitas/GShade-Shaders)
by Marot / Mortalitas, subject to per-file licenses in each shader header.

Injector engine is based on ReShade by Patrick Mours (crosire):
https://github.com/crosire/reshade (BSD-3-Clause).

Block is independent and not affiliated with GShade, ReShade, or Linden Lab.
